Man: IT Help Desk, this is Tom speaking. How can I assist you?

Woman: Hi Tom, this is Lisa from Marketing. My laptop won't connect to the office Wi-Fi this morning. I've tried restarting it twice already.

Man: I'm sorry to hear that. Let's try a few things. Can you check if Wi-Fi is turned on in your settings?

Woman: Yes, it's on, but it just shows "connecting" and never finishes.

Man: Okay, that sounds like it could be a network driver issue. Could you come by the IT office on the third floor? It might be faster to fix it in person.

Woman: Sure, I can head over now. Should I bring my laptop charger too?

Man: Yes, please bring it, just in case we need to run some updates that could take a while.

Woman: Got it, I'll be there in five minutes. Thanks, Tom.
